# Maintained distribution notice

This distribution is derived from the PASTE project by Max Land and the
Raphael Lab. The original source is available at
https://github.com/raphael-group/paste and remains covered by the BSD 3-Clause
license in `LICENSE`.

The maintained distribution keeps the registration and coordinate-stacking
runtime used by ChatSpatial, delegates fused Gromov-Wasserstein optimization to
the current public POT API, and omits plotting and notebook-only dependencies.
